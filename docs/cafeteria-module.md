# Cafeteria Module

Cafeteria eligibility, subsidy and billing follow the [cafeteria policy architecture](cafeteria-policy-architecture.md):

- the **employee organization** (from the assignment effective on the service date) owns the entitlement and pays;
- the **scanned cafeteria** is the service location; its **provider** is paid;
- an organization may eat only in networks and locations it has **explicit access** to ([network access](cafeteria-network-access.md));
- money comes only from that organization's approved, dated **service policy** — never from the cafeteria or a global setting;
- one entitlement is consumed once across every branch ([entitlement rules](cafeteria-entitlement-rules.md));
- settlements and reports use each transaction's applied snapshot ([settlement rules](cafeteria-settlement-rules.md)).

Functional reporting to a bureau, sharing a policy, or a cafeteria "primarily serving" an organization never grants access by itself.

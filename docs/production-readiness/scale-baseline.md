# Scale baseline

Production Gate 2, 2026-10-19/20. Everything below is measured: `loadtest/db/baseline.sql` on
each database. No production database was read or written.

## The 3M synthetic dataset

Generated by `php artisan scale:generate --employees=2996000 --organizations=300
--units-per-org=40 --history-days=2 --login-users=300` (plus two smoke runs of 1,000 and 3,000).

- **Runtime:** 7,753 s (2 h 9 min) for employees plus two days of history. The generator bulk
  inserts in chunks of 5,000, split into statements of 2,000 rows (PostgreSQL's 65,535-parameter
  limit).
- **Varied names:** `loadtest/db/realistic-names.sql` (32 min). The first generator version
  produced only 16 distinct names, which made every name search match 1/16 of the table; the
  generator now writes these names itself.
- **Entitlements:** `loadtest/db/backfill-entitlements.sql` added one cafeteria entitlement per
  employee (6 min).

Realism built in:

- **Every employee is fully linked:** organization → unit → position → current assignment →
  live card.
- **Suspended:** 1 in 40 employees, card suspended too.
- **History:** 1 in 3 has a closed earlier assignment; 1 in 4 a replaced earlier card.
- **Vacancies:** 1 in 20 positions is vacant.
- **Names:** about one distinct name per person, in English (`name_en`) and in Ge'ez
  (`full_name`), as on the live register.
- **No orphans:** the post-load integrity checks (below) are all zero.

Indexes are larger than the heap on `employees`, `id_cards` and `positions`.

- The biggest single indexes are `id_cards_token_hash_unique` (445 MB, never scanned during the
  benchmark: card verification goes by primary key and then compares the hash) and
  `positions_org_unit_code_idx` (358 MB).
- See [database-capacity.md](database-capacity.md#write-amplification) for the review of
  low-value indexes.
- The new indexes from this gate add 558 MB, already counted in the 13 GB above.

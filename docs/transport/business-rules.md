# Transport — Business Rules

1. **Verified usage only.** A verified boarding is an accepted, unvoided `transport_transactions` row on a trip that was not cancelled or failed. Reports, analytics and statements count nothing else.
2. **Route ≠ trip ≠ vehicle.** Planning lives on schedules and routes; evidence lives on trips.
3. **Trip authority.** By default (`require_trip_for_boarding`) a scan needs a trip that is `boarding` or `in_progress`; the trip must be today's, on the route the card is scanned for, of the scanning provider. Employees may join at later stops, so `in_progress` still boards.
4. **Eligibility basis**, in order: active route assignment (pass) → employee grant → organization grant (when `organization_grants_board_without_pass`) → pay-as-you-go (when allowed; flagged, not verified usage for settlement) → not required (when `require_pass_for_scan` is off). The basis is snapshotted on the transaction.
5. **Alternative route** policy: `deny` (default), `allow_same_provider`, `allow_any_eligible`. An allowed alternative boarding is flagged `alternative_route` in the snapshot and raises a `wrong_route` info exception when refused.
6. **Duplicates.** One accepted boarding per employee per trip is enforced by the application and by a partial unique index; `duplicate_boarding_policy` additionally refuses a second boarding in the same direction on another trip the same day (`already_boarded_this_leg`). A resent request with the same idempotency key / nonce is answered with the first result and never stored twice.
7. **Capacity.** The effective capacity is the trip's capacity or the vehicle's approved capacity. `capacity_enforcement = block` refuses `trip_full`; `warn` accepts and records an `overcapacity` warning exception. Either way the evidence is kept.
8. **Manual exception.** Only with `manual_exception_enabled` and the operator's manual permission; it names the employee by number and needs a reason; it is flagged (`verification_method = manual_exception`), audited, counted on the manifest and raises `excessive_manual_exceptions` above the threshold.
9. **Void, never edit.** A boarding is voided with a reason by staff with `transport-transactions.void`, within scope, and never once it sits on an approved statement. The row stays, the trip count drops, the manifest shows it voided.
10. **Trips.** A trip needs a vehicle to start boarding; cancellation and failure need a reason, notify assigned employees and keep any boarding evidence (not settled). A vehicle may be replaced mid-trip: earlier boardings stay on the first vehicle, later ones go to the second; a `vehicle_substituted` exception records it.
11. **Transfers and separations.** A new current assignment or a status change re-evaluates every active route assignment (observer + job): routes that no longer serve the employee's organization are suspended with a reason and the employee is told; a separated employee is refused at once.
12. **Revoked / inactive card.** Refused with the card's state as the reason; past boardings remain.
13. **Contracts.** Versioned; a version is approved by someone other than its drafter; a statement snapshots the version it applied, so later versions change nothing historical. A model's required rates must be present; the system never supplies a rate (`NEEDS_POLICY_DECISION_TRANSPORT_TARIFF`).
14. **Invoice ≠ verified ≠ approved.** The claim, the computed amount, the adjustment total and the approved amount are separate columns; none overwrites another. Reconciliation shows claimed vs verified vs calculated vs adjusted vs approved.
15. **Maker-checker.** The person who computed a statement cannot approve it (`settlement_requires_distinct_approver`); the requester of an adjustment cannot decide it; pending adjustments and disputes block approval.
16. **Organization scope everywhere** for staff; **provider isolation everywhere** for the portal and the device API.
17. **Not attendance, not payroll, not discipline.** No-show is a utilization figure, never a finding about a person.
18. **Privacy.** The provider tier sees name and number; organization appears only on the staff tier; no national ID, no HR data, no employee GPS.
19. **Offline never fails open.** Offline boardings are refused unless the feature flag and the setting allow them; each synced record is re-verified against the trip's date and grace window, stays `offline_pending` until reconciled, and settlement flags any still pending.
20. **Bounded queries.** Every list paginates; reports are bounded to one year per request; exports stream.

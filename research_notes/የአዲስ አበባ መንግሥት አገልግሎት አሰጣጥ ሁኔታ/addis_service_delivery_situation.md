# Public service delivery in Addis Ababa City Administration government institutions, 2023–2026 (ነባራዊ ሁኔታ and problem statement inputs)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Several news sites (addisstandard.com, parts of addisfortune.news, allafrica.com) blocked direct fetching. Where a figure comes only from a search-engine excerpt of a blocked page, the note says so. Ethiopian-calendar dates are converted where the source gives both.

## Q1. Structure: sub-cities, woredas, executive bodies, population

### Takeaway
The governing legal instrument is Proclamation No. 84/2023 (አዋጅ ቁጥር 84/2016), dated 26 November 2023 (ኅዳር 15 ቀን 2016 ዓ.ም.). It re-establishes 48 city executive organs: the Mayor's Office, 21 bureaus, 5 commissions, 11 authorities, 1 corporation, 5 agencies and 4 other bodies. The city has 11 sub-cities since Lemi Kura was added in 2020. The last documented woreda count is 116, from 2020 and before the Lemi Kura change; no current official count was found. Population estimates range from about 3.9 million to 6.7 million depending on the source and the boundary used.

### Cited Findings
- **Proclamation No. 84/2023**, "A Proclamation to Provide for the Re-establishment of the Executive Organs of the Addis Ababa City Government" (Amharic: «የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር አስፈፃሚ አካላትን እንደገና ለማቋቋም የወጣ አዋጅ ቁጥር 84/2016»). Published in Addis Negari Gazeta, 31st Year No. 84, 26 November 2023. It was issued under the revised City Charter, Proclamation No. 361/2003 (as amended). — [Addis Ababa City Administration, Proclamation 84/2023 PDF (file uploaded 2025-02-06)](https://addisababappp.addismayor.gov.et/uploads/Publication/addis-ababa-city-administration-a-decree-issued-to-deal-with-the-bodies-again-2025-02-06-67a4c4dcc23be.pdf)

- **Three tiers of administration.** The proclamation defines the sub-city (ክፍለ ከተማ) as the "second administrative stratum" and the woreda (ወረዳ) as the "third administrative stratum of the city, which is a unit of a sub-city". - **Population: UN agglomeration figure.** UN World Population Prospects 2024, via Worldometers, gives 6,705,705 for the 2025 urban agglomeration. This uses a different boundary from the administrative city. — [Worldometers](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/ethiopia-population/)
- **Population: older administrative-region figure.** A geo-ref.net table gives 3,859,999 for the Addis Ababa region in 2025 (526 km²). This is likely derived from the CSA/ESS projection series. — [geo-ref.net](https://www.geo-ref.net/pdf/ethiopia.pdf)
- **ESS context.** The Ethiopian Statistical Service put the national population at 109.4 million in July 2024, far below UN figures. No ESS city-level 2024/25 figure was retrieved. — [The Reporter](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/44144/)
- **Older bureau count (2019).** An earlier reshuffle cut city bureaus and offices from 71 to 45. — [Addis Fortune, 2019](https://addisfortune.news/?p=24101)

### Inferences
- For the proposal, the "48 executive organs + 11 sub-cities + woredas" structure (Proclamation 84/2023) is the citable institutional frame. The platform would need to serve three tiers: city bureaus and authorities, sub-cities, and woredas.
- Population: quote a range and name its source. For example, "about 5.9 million (2024 projection, C40/AACA)" or "6.7 million urban agglomeration (UN WPP 2024)". Do not present a single number as "official".

### Gaps
- No official current woreda count after the 2020 Lemi Kura restructuring was found. Searches for 119, 120 and 121 returned nothing. Verify with the City Administration or the Council.
- It is unknown whether Proclamation 84/2023 has been amended since 2023. The PDF was uploaded in February 2025.
- The Addis Ababa Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission and the city Auditor General do not appear among the 48 executive organs. They are presumably accountable to the Council, but this was not verified.

## Q2. Workforce and the PSHRD Bureau's mandate and HR reforms

### Takeaway
The only workforce figure found is "more than 163,000 employees" across all levels, reported around December 2023. A December 2023 city competency assessment passed only about 43% of 15,151 examinees, and only about a third of leaders. Proclamation 84/2023 explicitly assigns the PSHRD Bureau several relevant duties:
- organize the city's HR information using technology
- set service standards
- run customer-satisfaction surveys
- run performance evaluation and merit-based placement

### Cited Findings
- **Workforce size.** "The Addis Abeba city administration oversees a workforce comprising more than 163,000 employees functioning at various levels within its organizational structure." The context is a civil-servant aptitude exam scheduled for 22 December 2023. Search excerpt only; the article returned HTTP 403. — [Addis Standard, c. Dec 2023](https://addisstandard.com/?p=40257)
- **December 2023 competency assessment results.** Search excerpts only; the article was blocked.
  - 15,151 candidates examined; 6,517 passed (43%).
  - Leaders: 34% (1,422) met the 60% minimum.
  - General staff: about 50% (5,095) passed.
  - 681 tests voided for procedural errors; 441 staff absent.
  - A second round was planned.
  
  — [Addis Standard, "Over 40% of city employees pass competency assessment"](https://addisstandard.com/news-over-40-of-city-employees-pass-competency-assessment/). Addis Fortune reported 44% of the 15,151 passed. — [Addis Fortune, Jan 2026](https://addisfortune.news/?p=45785)
- **New certification institute.** The City Cabinet approved a "Civil Servants Qualification & Certification Institute" together with the 2026/27 budget of 502 billion Birr. The page carries no date; it is probably June 2026. — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/cabinet-approves-502b-birr-budget-as-city-returns-to-federal-subsidy)
- **PSHRD Bureau duties under Proclamation 84/2023, Art. 22(2).** — [Proclamation 84/2023](https://addisababappp.addismayor.gov.et/uploads/Publication/addis-ababa-city-administration-a-decree-issued-to-deal-with-the-bodies-again-2025-02-06-67a4c4dcc23be.pdf)
  - (5) Ensure city offices' services have appropriate standards; make offices prepare them; approve and enforce them. Amharic: «በከተማው አስተዳደር መስሪያ ቤቶች የሚሰጡ አገልግሎቶች ተገቢዉ ስታንዳርድ መኖሩን ያረጋግጣል፤ እንዲያዘጋጁ ያደርጋል፤ ያጸድቃል».
  - (6) Monitor service delivery performance and conduct customer-satisfaction studies. Amharic: «የአገልግሎት አሰጣጥ አፈጻፀም ይከታተላል፣ የተገልጋዩን እርካታ ጥናት ያካሂዳል».
  - (7) Ensure a transparent and accountable complaint system for public servants.
  - (9) Establish a performance-evaluation system for government employees.
  - (10) Establish competence- and performance-based placement, promotion, step increment, benefits and incentives.
  - (12) Issue directives on recruitment, placement, promotion, transfer, allowances, training and discipline.
  - **(14) "Organize the human resource information of the city government using modern technology".** Amharic: «የከተማ አስተዳደሩን የሰዉ ሀብት መረጃዎችን በቴክኖሎጂ በመታገዝ ያደራጃል».
  - (15) Build a public-servant mindset and a competency-assurance system.
- **Older context (2019).** Placement used periodic merit ratings rather than exams; plan execution and work ethic were 70% of the points. Over 54,000 city workers took part in a BPR evaluation. — [Addis Fortune, 2019](https://addisfortune.news/?p=23878); [Capital Ethiopia tag page](https://capitalethiopia.com/tag/addis-ababa-city-public-service/)
- **Salaries.** Mid-year 2025/26 salary revisions for civil servants were made to offset inflation after the Birr was floated. No figures were given. — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/cabinet-approves-502b-birr-budget-as-city-returns-to-federal-subsidy)

### Inferences
- Duty (14) on technology-supported HR information, together with duties (5) and (6) on standards and satisfaction surveys, gives the proposal a direct legal mandate. An integrated employee-management and service platform is a way for the Bureau to carry out its existing statutory duties, not a new mandate.
- The 2023 competency results (only 34% of leaders passed) can be cited as evidence of a capacity gap. They do not by themselves describe the state of HR records.

### Gaps
- No official 2024–2026 headcount from the PSHRD Bureau was found. The 163,000 figure is from 2023 and was seen only in search excerpts.
- No Addis Ababa "ghost employee" or payroll/HR audit findings were found in English searches.
- No evidence was found of an existing city HRMIS, digital personnel file system or city employee ID project.
- The results of the second competency round were not found.

## Q3. Documented service-delivery problems

### Takeaway
Official and media sources from 2024–2026 document the same set of problems:
- land-related fraud and corruption
- forged documents in paper-based processes
- slow and frustrating services
- maladministration and theft

Leaders frame these as the reason for Mesob and for digitization. Hard numbers are scarce: no city-level complaint counts or satisfaction percentages from 2023–2026 were found. Academic studies, mostly 2019–2021, consistently report low customer satisfaction in sub-city offices.

### Cited Findings
- **Prime Minister at the Mesob launch (April 2025):** "We know the public is tired of slow and frustrating services... Land issues and corruption have been sources of deep dissatisfaction." — [Addis Fortune, c. Nov 2025](https://addisfortune.news/mesob-pushes-digital-platform-to-meet-entrenched-resistance)
- **Same article on bureaucracy and accountability.** It describes Mesob as a salvo "against a deeply entrenched bureaucracy and widespread public discontent". A legal consultant quoted there warns that digitization without re-engineering, frontline training and accountability "risk[s] becoming window dressing". — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/?p=42968)
- **Mayor on Addis Mesob.** Mayor Adanech Abiebie tied the Addis Mesob reform to theft and maladministration. Search excerpt only; the page is behind a browser check. — [Addis Fortune (centres tag)](https://addisfortune.news/tag/centres)
- **Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission (April 2024).** Its nine-month Rapid Corruption Prevention report ranked land-related services second (after procurement) as the sector most prone to corruption in several regions, Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa. Most land cases involve urban land lease. The schemes it prevented were worth close to 700 million Birr nationally. — [The Reporter, 14 Apr 2024](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/39625/)
- **Mayor on land fraud (July 2026).**
  - The paper land-map process was "vulnerable to forgery".
  - The city has moved to a fully digital e-map, digitized all existing land titles with scanned backups, and extended digitization to the revenue authority and the construction-permit office.
  - Officials are prioritizing the land services "that generate the most public complaints".
  - A cabinet regulation allows automatic dismissal of employees implicated in land misconduct.
  
  — [The Reporter, 18 Jul 2026](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51911/)
- **Earlier pattern (2022).** The city ordered woreda officials to serve property owners only in person and restricted power-of-attorney transactions, citing fraud. Residents objected, and the restriction was partly relaxed. This illustrates repeat-visit requirements driven by fraud risk. — [Addis Fortune, "City backs down on land transactions"](https://addisfortune.news/city-backs-down-on-land-transactions)
- **Federal Ombudsman (2016, older).** Reported widespread maladministration in Addis Ababa in subsidized-goods distribution, housing and construction, land management and compensation. — [The Reporter, 18 Jun 2016](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/4254/)
- **Academic studies (Addis Ababa University theses):**
  - **Gulele sub-city (2019):** 400 respondents, SERVQUAL. "Majority of customer are not satisfied with the current delivery system"; no percentage given. — [Tsigu Aklilu, AAU, May 2019](https://etd.aau.edu.et/items/a51cc5ca-71f8-4e3d-932d-383d1adb9817/full)
  - **Lideta branch, city Revenues Bureau (2021):** 390 respondents from 14,920 taxpayers, SERVQUAL. Customer satisfaction was "minimal"; responsiveness, assurance and empathy had the strongest effects. — [Aregay Abrehet, AAU, Sept 2021](https://etd.aau.edu.et/items/11259a8d-644f-41f3-923b-83c8ead76be3)
  - **Other theses, findings not retrieved:**
    - Kirkos sub-city civil registration and vital events customer satisfaction (July 2023)
    - Woreda 02, Nifas Silk Lafto: vital-events service delivery and satisfaction
    - Yeka sub-city land holding registration service quality (2019)
    - Addis Ketema revenue office (2019)
    
    — [AAU ETD search results](https://etd.aau.edu.et/items/4ba1d3c6-4deb-40bf-a05b-52d911d87243/full); [Gulele/Kirkos listing](https://etd.aau.edu.et/bitstreams/07df4663-6f13-4036-81f6-fd1e08ba3250/download)
  - **Bole sub-city citizen's charter (2021):** a study of the charter's achievements and challenges and of satisfaction after its introduction. — [AAU ETD](https://etd.aau.edu.et/items/726a5400-7222-4006-9d40-47b6ba016e17/full)
- **J-PAL study (2016–2017), Addis Ababa outskirts and surrounding Oromia zones, not the city administration itself:**
  - Only 35% of administrators could name at least one of their community's top two service priorities.
  - About 24% of respondents had complained to a local official about electricity; only about a fifth of those complaints were resolved.
  
  — [J-PAL evaluation](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/role-information-accountability-and-resource-gaps-explaining-poor-urban-services-quality)

### Inferences
- The best-sourced problem statements are:
  - Leaders admit public frustration with slow services and with land-related corruption (PM 2025, Mayor 2025–2026).
  - The EACC ranks land services among the most corruption-prone in Addis Ababa (2024).
  - Paper-based records enabled forgery (Mayor 2026).
  - Academic studies repeatedly find low satisfaction in sub-city offices.
- Brokers (ደላላ), queues and repeated visits are widely discussed. In this research they were documented only indirectly, through the 2022 in-person rule and the fraud and forgery coverage.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 city-level complaint counts were found from the City Council, the Addis Ababa Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission, the Ombudsman's Addis branch, or the city Auditor General. These sources are likely published in Amharic on Facebook or Telegram, or in council session reports, that search did not index.
- No quantified broker, queue or repeat-visit data for Addis Ababa institutions was found.
- Search excerpts attributed statements about government workers colluding with brokers in land deals to the Mayor and the PM. The exact source pages were not verified, so those statements are not cited here.

## Q4. Sectors with the most public complaints

### Takeaway
Land administration is consistently named as the top complaint and corruption sector: by the PM in 2025, by the EACC in 2024 and by the Mayor in 2026. Housing, compensation and revenue/tax services follow. No quantitative ranking was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Land:** second most corruption-prone sector in Addis Ababa, after procurement; urban land lease cases dominate. — [The Reporter, 14 Apr 2024](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/39625/)
- **Land:** officials are prioritizing for digitization the land services that "generate the most public complaints". — [The Reporter, 18 Jul 2026](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51911/)
- **Land and corruption** named as "sources of deep dissatisfaction" (PM, April 2025). — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/mesob-pushes-digital-platform-to-meet-entrenched-resistance)
- **Revenue/tax:** the Mayor's Office launched a dedicated tax complaint system on 4 December 2025, aimed at taxpayers dissatisfied with revenue collection, staff conduct or service delivery. — [Addis Fortune (mayors tag)](https://addisfortune.news/tag/mayors)
- **Housing, land, compensation and subsidized goods** (Ombudsman, 2016, older). — [The Reporter, 2016](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/4254/)

### Inferences
- The proposal can state that land, housing, revenue and civil-registration services are the highest-friction, most-complained-about areas. It should cite the qualitative sources above rather than invent counts.

### Gaps
- No complaint statistics by sector for 2023–2026 were found from city bodies.

## Q5. City reform and digitalization initiatives, 2023–2026

### Takeaway
The flagship reform is Mesob (መሶብ). The federal Mesob centre opened in Addis Ababa in April 2025. The city then rolled out its own "Addis Mesob" one-stop digital government service centres in sub-cities:
- 6 by an undated report
- the 8th, in Kolfe Keranyo, on 4 June 2026
- 11 centres with more than 150 digitized services by July 2026

Other city reforms include:
- digitized land titles and e-maps
- e-procurement in 61 institutions
- an asset database
- an e-school portal
- online complaint channels: a Mayor's Office good-governance portal and the 9977 tax hotline

No city HR digitalization project was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Federal Mesob launch.** The PM inaugurated it in April 2025 as a pilot integrating 12 federal agencies. — [Shega](https://shega.co/news/pm-abiy-bets-on-mesob-ethiopias-latest-push-for-a-paperless-public-sector); [allAfrica (FR), Apr 2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202504270081.html)
- **Federal Mesob growth (c. Nov 2025).** It grew "from 22 to 124 services in six months, onboarding 11 institutions". It operates out of Gerji and serves "200 clients daily". It planned 24-hour, three-shift operation funded by a 5% service surcharge. A draft regulation on "Establishment of a One-Stop Digital Government Service" went to the Council of Ministers. — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/?p=42968)
- **National claims (July 2026, Civil Service Commissioner).** Mesob became operational on 26 April 2025; there are 106 centres nationwide; citizen satisfaction is "98.8 percent". These are official, unverified claims; search excerpt. — [ENA via allAfrica, 8 Jul 2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202607090662.html)
  - **Conflicting count:** Report.az (March 2026) said 36 ASAN-model centres were operating in Ethiopia. — [Report.az](https://report.az/en/foreign-politics/new-service-centers-opened-in-ethiopia-based-on-asan-service-model)
- **MESOB board and scope.** MESOB ("Modern Ethiopia Service for Organized Benefits") delivers more than 130 services, authenticated by Fayda national ID. Its board held its first meeting on 25 May 2026. — [Kana TV](https://kanatelevision.com/news/ethiopia-s-mesob-which-now-delivers-130-government-services-from-one-centre-holds-its-first/)
- **Addis Mesob rollout, earlier report.** Launched in six sub-cities, with five to follow within the fiscal year; each centre offers about 150 services, with plans to double capacity. Search excerpt; undated. — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/tag/centres)
- **Addis Mesob, 8th centre (official source).** Inaugurated in Kolfe Keranyo sub-city on 4 June 2026 by Mayor Adanech Abiebie: a seven-storey building with 96 service windows, 22 institutions and 114 digital services. Stated aim: "fair, transparent, and efficient public service delivery". — [Addis Ababa Mayor's Office, Week in Review](https://addismayor.gov.et/blogs/detail/645)
- **City five-year results (July 2026):**
  - 11 One-Window (Mesob) centres with more than 150 digitized services, credited with reducing malpractice and improving accountability
  - e-procurement in 61 institutions
  - 6,815 government buildings (assets worth over 87 billion Birr) in an asset database
  - 2025/26 budget of 360.07 billion Birr; revenue of 350 billion Birr
  
  — [Ethiopian Business Review, 13 Jul 2026](https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/?p=17707)
- **Land digitization:** all existing land titles digitized; e-map in operation; extended to the revenue authority and construction permits. — [The Reporter, 18 Jul 2026](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/51911/)
- **Complaint channels:**
  - The Mayor's Office Good Governance Complaint and Public Feedback portal accepts reports of delays, misconduct, corruption and abuse of authority. It supports anonymous submission and up to 8 evidence files, and gives a tracking number. No response-time standard is stated. — [addismayor.gov.et complaint portal](https://nifaslink.addismayor.gov.et/customer/complaints)
  - Tax complaint free call centre **9977** and a confidential complaint box, operational from 4 December 2025. — [Addis Fortune](https://addisfortune.news/tag/mayors)
- **Smart-city and education.** The city Innovation Bureau's e-school portal is being extended to all public schools, "paving the way for citywide digital registration" (May 2025). Search excerpts add more than 500 schools integrated and about 2.2 billion Birr allocated to ICT for the smart-city push; these were not seen in the fetched text. — [Shega, 22 May 2025](https://shega.co/news/smart-city-push-e-school-portal-to-cover-all-public-schools-in-addis-ababa-by-next-year)
- **National strategy.** Digital Ethiopia 2030 was launched in December 2025. A state minister said online public services had "recently been launched in Addis Ababa, Adama, Bishoftu, Hawassa". — [ENA via allAfrica, 21 Dec 2025](https://allafrica.com/stories/202512220128.html)

### Inferences
- Mesob solves the citizen-facing front office, with one window and digital services. It does not, on its own evidence, address the back office of the city's own workforce: HR records, performance, placement and accountability of the employees who deliver those services. The proposal can position the PSHRD platform as complementary to Mesob.
